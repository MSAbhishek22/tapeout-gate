What happens onchain

Only three things ever leave this desk: the payload hash, the verdict, and the receipt id. They are written to the GateAudit contract on X Layer, and the destination is checked against the PolicyRegistry. The payload itself — and any secret inside it — stays offchain, in the receipt’s masked form only.
